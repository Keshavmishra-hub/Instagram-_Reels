Only two countries made more solar power than India.

That's 2025 data from Ember, compiled by Our World in Data. Rewind to 2015. Solar supplied just 0.54% of India's electricity.

Its solar plants generated about 7 terawatt-hours that year. By 2025, that had climbed to about 196 terawatt-hours. Solar's share of India's power rose to 9.4%.

The only two countries ahead of India? China and the United States.

Still, coal produced about 71% of India's electricity, and renewables about 24%.
